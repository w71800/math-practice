# 第一部分：特殊角的三角函數值

## 練習 1：30°－60°－90°三角形

已知直角三角形如下：

- 最短邊 = 1  
- 斜邊 = 2  
- 另一股 = √3  

### (1)

對於 30°

:::question
sin30° = {{blank}}

cos30° = {{blank}}

tan30° = {{blank}}

answer:
\sin 30° = \frac{1}{2},\quad \cos 30° = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\quad \tan 30° = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}
:::

---

### (2)

對於 60°

:::question
sin60° = {{blank}}

cos60° = {{blank}}

tan60° = {{blank}}

answer:
\sin 60° = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\quad \cos 60° = \frac{1}{2},\quad \tan 60° = \sqrt{3}
:::

---

## 練習 2：45°－45°－90°三角形

已知：

- 兩股長皆為 1  
- 斜邊為 √2  

:::question
sin45° = {{blank}}  
cos45° = {{blank}}  
tan45° = {{blank}}

answer:
\sin 45° = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \\
\cos 45° = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \\
\tan 45° = 1
:::

## 練習 3：特殊角值填空

:::question
| θ   | sinθ | cosθ | tanθ |
|-----|------|------|------|
| 30° | {{blank}} | {{blank}} | {{blank}} |
| 45° | {{blank}} | {{blank}} | {{blank}} |
| 60° | {{blank}} | {{blank}} | {{blank}} |
| 90° | {{blank}} | {{blank}} | {{blank}} |

answer:
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
\theta & \sin\theta & \cos\theta & \tan\theta \\
\hline
30° & \dfrac{1}{2} & \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} & \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \\
45° & \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} & \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} & 1 \\
60° & \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} & \dfrac{1}{2} & \sqrt{3} \\
90° & 1 & 0 & \text{無定義}
\end{array}
:::

# 第三部分：廣義角分析流程練習

請依照以下步驟：

1. 找出相關銳角  
2. 判斷所在象限  
3. 判斷使用定義  
   - sin → y/r  
   - cos → x/r  
   - tan → y/x  
4. 判斷正負  
5. 求出函數值
